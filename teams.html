teams in the nba

The NBA is made up of 30 teams divided into two conferences, the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference. Some of the biggest teams include the Boston Celtics, Los Angeles Lakers, Golden State Warriors, Oklahoma City Thunder, Denver Nuggets, and Milwaukee Bucks. The Oklahoma City Thunder are led by superstar Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, while the Denver Nuggets rely on Nikola Jokić's incredible scoring and passing ability. The Los Angeles Lakers have Luka Dončić leading their offence, and the Golden State Warriors continue to be known for Stephen Curry's amazing three-point shooting. Other competitive teams include the New York Knicks, Cleveland Cavaliers, Minnesota Timberwolves, Houston Rockets, and Philadelphia 76ers. Every team is fighting to win games, improve its position in the standings, and eventually compete for the NBA Championship.

team records.

In the NBA, the playoffs are the final part of the season where the best teams compete to become NBA champions. There are two conferences, the Eastern Conference and the Western Conference, and each has 15 teams. During the regular season, every team plays 82 games, and their records are based on how many games they win and lose. For example, a record of 50–32 means a team won 50 games and lost 32. Teams are ranked based on their win-loss records, with the top six teams in each conference automatically qualifying for the playoffs, while teams ranked 7th to 10th compete in a Play-In Tournament for the final two playoff spots.

the playoffs

Once the playoffs begin, teams are ranked from 1 to 8 in each conference, with the higher-ranked teams facing lower-ranked teams. Each playoff round is a best-of-seven series, meaning the first team to win four games moves on to the next round. There are four rounds: the First Round, Conference Semifinals, Conference Finals, and finally the NBA Finals, where the Eastern Conference champion plays the Western Conference champion to decide who wins the NBA championship. Having a better regular-season record is important because it usually gives a team home-court advantage, meaning they get to play more games at their own arena if the series goes to seven games.
